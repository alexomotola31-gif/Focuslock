FocusLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

FocusLock ("the app") helps you stay focused by locking apps you choose, either for a timer you start or on a schedule you set. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone and is never sent to us or to any server we control:

  • The list of apps you choose to lock
  • Your lock schedules, timer settings, goals and session history
  • Emergency codes generated for manual timer sessions
  • Your appearance settings (theme, accent color, text size)

We do not run a FocusLock account system or a server that receives this data. Uninstalling the app removes it.

2. Accessibility Service

FocusLock uses Android's Accessibility Service for one purpose: to detect which app is currently in the foreground so it can show the lock screen when you open an app you chose to lock. The service does not read, record or store the content of your screen, what you type, passwords, messages or any other personal content. Information from the service is not shared with anyone. You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility, but locking will then stop working.

3. Other permissions

  • Display over other apps / usage access (where requested): to show the lock screen on top of a locked app.
  • Notifications: for session reminders and pre-lock warnings.
  • Battery optimization exemption: so Android does not stop FocusLock from running in the background and the lock keeps working.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

FocusLock shows banner ads on its Home screen when no focus session is active. Ads are never shown on the lock screen or during setup. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your locked-app list, schedules and session data are never shared with advertisers.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads that use personal data are requested. You can change your choice later where the app offers a privacy-options setting. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google, for advertising as described above.

6. Children

FocusLock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we do not transmit your focus data off your device.

8. Your choices

  • Delete all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Disable the Accessibility Service in Android Settings.
  • Reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or change ad consent where the app offers it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
