# FocusLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

FocusLock helps you stay focused by locking apps you choose during timed sessions and schedules.

## Short version
FocusLock keeps all of its data on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no internet access. We do not collect, receive, or share your data.

## What is stored on your device
- The apps you choose to lock (their package names)
- Your schedules, goals, Pomodoro, notification, sound and appearance settings
- A history of your focus sessions (start time, length, and whether it finished), used for Insights, goals and streaks
- Your emergency code, stored only as a salted hash in Android's encrypted storage. The code itself is shown once and is never stored
- Daily counts of emergency unlocks and schedule skips

This data stays in the app's private storage. Uninstalling FocusLock or clearing its storage deletes it.

## Permissions and why they are used
- **Accessibility service:** used only to notice which app has just opened, so FocusLock can show its lock screen when you open an app you chose to lock. It does not read what is on your screen, what you type, passwords, or messages, and nothing it sees leaves your phone. You are asked for consent before it is enabled, and you can turn it off in Android settings at any time.
- **Device administrator:** used only so FocusLock cannot be uninstalled in the middle of a focus session. You can switch it off in Android settings when no session is running.
- **Notifications:** used for session, break, goal and upcoming-schedule alerts. You can switch each kind off. Notifications never show your emergency code.
- **Run at startup:** used to set your schedule reminders again after the phone restarts.
- **Battery settings:** FocusLock only opens Android's battery settings screen so you can keep it running in the background. It does not change any setting itself.
- **App list:** FocusLock reads the list of launchable apps on your phone so you can choose which to lock. The list is not sent anywhere.

## Sharing
FocusLock does not send data to anyone. It has no internet permission.

## Children
FocusLock is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
